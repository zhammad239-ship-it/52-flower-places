Simple Flower Care Tips

Keep Your Blooms Fresh Longer

01
Keep flowers away from direct heat and warm drafts.
02
Keep the floral arrangement in a clean, dust-free environment.
03
Refresh water regularly when applicable to maintain cleanliness.
04
Trim stems slightly at an angle when needed to boost absorption.
05
Keep flowers away from harsh, strong direct sunlight.
